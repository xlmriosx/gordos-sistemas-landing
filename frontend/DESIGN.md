## How to read this file

This file carries **two systems, in this order**.

1. **The product** — Obsidian & Gold Cypher-Order, the brand of the Gordos
   Sistemas landing in [`app/landing/`](app/landing/). It is the first half of
   this file, and it is the one a screen in `app/` follows by default.
2. **The kit** — the shared design system in [`src/`](src/), the second half. A
   different brand with a different job: portable, with a light and a dark
   theme, and ~90 components that depend on its tokens.

The two never meet in the code. The landing scopes its own tokens to
`.landing` in `app/landing/landing.css`; the kit keeps its own in
`src/styles/colors_and_type.css`. When a value differs between the halves,
the half that owns the surface wins: the landing's tokens for anything inside
`.landing`, the kit's for anything else.

**The stitch's artifacts** live in `stitch_gordos_sistemas_landing_page/`
(untracked in this repo): `code.html` is the exported page, `DESIGN.md` is the
brand book this file starts from, `view.png` the full page, `screen.png` the
marble banner, `logo.png` the lion. Where the brand book and the exported page
disagree, **the exported page wins** — it is what the page actually renders.

**The radii above are the four the page renders** (0.125 / 0.25 / 0.5 /
0.75rem, the design's own `rounded` / `rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl` /
`rounded-full`). The stitch's brand book carried a Material-style scale of six
that the exported design never used, and its `full` was a real circle where the
design's is a capsule.

## Brand & Style

This design system defines an austere, solemn, and sovereign aesthetic crafted for a collective of Argentine technologists championing individual freedom, cryptography, and open systems. 

The aesthetic fuses classical stonework, imperial iconography, and esoteric lodge traditions with cutting-edge cypherpunk precision. It merges the gravitas of carved Roman marble slabs with the luminescent, high-tech rigor of cryptographic terminals. 

Visually, the system evokes discipline, intellectual autonomy, and unyielding strength. It steers away from playful tech tropes, adopting deep obsidian canvases, chiseled gold filigree, warm candle-amber ambient illumination, and mystical violet depths.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an unyielding dark environment, reminiscent of polished black marble and dark obsidian chambers, elevated by antique gold leaf and luminous amber.

- **Primary (`#d4af37`)**: Imperial gold. Used for primary interactive actions, high-significance states, glowing perimeter strokes, and authoritative badges.
- **Secondary (`#7c3aed`)**: Deep arcane violet. Represents cryptographic secrecy, sovereignty, and background atmospheric depth. Used for secondary actions, deep glow effects, and specialized technical indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#f59e0b`)**: Radiant amber flame. Applied to warnings, critical telemetry, live node pulses, and focused hover accents.
- **Neutral (`#0a0a0c`)**: True obsidian black with undertones of dark stone (`#13111c`). Provides the foundational backdrop across all viewport surfaces.

Secondary surface layers utilize `#181524` and `#221d33` to structure containers, while text spans between polished silver-white (`#f3f4f6`) and muted slate gold (`#a39b82`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges classical monumental inscriptions and developer precision:

- **Headlines & Mottos (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys classical solemnity, historical authority, and architectural permanence. Used for manifesto titles, hero slogans, section dividers, and formal proclamations. Display styles benefit from slight letter-spacing and uppercase styling where appropriate.
- **Interface & Reading (`Inter`)**: Guarantees optimal legibility across complex dashboards, collective documents, and long-form political or technical texts. Neutral, balanced, and invisible.
- **Code & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Powers smart contract data, hashes, network nodes, metrics, terminal outputs, and interactive status tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements an architectural 12-column fluid grid on desktop viewports (collapsing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile devices). 

Spacing is disciplined and deliberate. Generous margins ground the UI in a stone-monument presentation, avoiding cluttered modern SaaS density. Containers prioritize clear vertical rhythm, allowing high-contrast typography and glowing artifacts room to assert presence. Alignment emphasizes strict bilateral or left-anchored geometry with central focal badges.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft drop shadows, but rather on luminance, subtle inner stone bevels, and atmospheric glow:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas sits on `#0a0a0c`. Elevated modules use `#13111c` layered with 1px border strokes tinted in `#382e1e` or semi-translucent gold `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- **Gilded Borders & Glow**: Active, focused, or sovereign elements feature sharp 1px borders in `#d4af37` accompanied by an ambient rim blur: `0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.3)`.
- **Mystic Violet Atmospherics**: Floating overlays, modals, and focal hero components cast a deep radial backdrop blur (`rgba(76, 29, 149, 0.25)`) to generate an illuminated halo reminiscent of candles against dark stone.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a tight, chiseled geometry (`roundedness: 1`), evoking carved black marble slabs, cryptographic hardware keys, and architectural stonework:

- Base elements (buttons, inputs, chips) carry a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Cards, containers, and dialog panels utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- Circles and capsules are reserved exclusively for status oracles, cryptographic seal badges, and avatar frames. Soft or bubble-like contours are strictly forbidden.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign**: Deep gold gradient background (`linear-gradient(180deg, #d4af37 0%, #b8860b 100%)`) with dark obsidian text (`#0a0a0c`), bold font weight, 1px gold highlight border, and a subtle amber perimeter flare on hover.
- **Secondary Ritual**: Obsidian-purple backdrop (`#13111c`), 1px gold border (`#d4af37`), and antique gold typography. Hovering triggers an inner glow (`inset 0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
- **Ghost Cypher**: Transparent base with JetBrains Mono monospace text, shifting to violet (`#7c3aed`) on hover with terminal brackets `[ ]` surrounding the text.

### Cards & Monolithic Panels
- Constructed from layered obsidian surfaces (`#13111c`) with razor-sharp 1px stone-gold borders.
- Header bands incorporate subtle horizontal hairline dividers and classical Playfair serif headings paired with monospace metadata tags.

### Input Fields
- Dark recesses (`#0a0a0c`) with sunken inner borders (`2a2638`). 
- Monospaced text input in `#f3f4f6`. Focused states illuminate the entire perimeter with a crisp `#d4af37` rim and warm amber glow.

### Badges & Chips
- Angular, compact indicators framed with 1px borders. 
- Status indicators combine uppercase `JetBrains Mono` text with a 6px glowing indicator dot representing node connectivity, cryptographic signatures, or sovereign rank.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp, square containers (4px radius) bordered in dark brass. 
- Selected state fills with solid gold `#d4af37` and displays an obsidian emblem checkmark.

## The kit underneath

The kit is a design system with one brand: **Sentient** (light, display) for
anything that should feel considered, **Geist** (and **Geist Mono** for
labels/numerals) for everything functional. The palette is almost monochrome —
carbon black and off-white do most of the work — with a small set of named
accents (dusty mauve, ice blue, lavender, sage) used sparingly rather than as
a rotating "brand color". It is editorial and quiet: hairlines instead of heavy
borders, square-leaning corners, soft low-contrast shadows, restrained motion.

It is also **portable**: `src/` is meant to drop into another project
unmodified, which is why it has a light and a dark theme and why the landing
does not touch it. The landing's brand replaces the kit's *at the product
layer*; the kit stays the kit it was promoted as. Its tokens are the `kit:`
block in the frontmatter above.

This half is not a restatement of the component library — that's
[`README.md`](README.md) §4, with ~80 components under `src/components/ui` and
`src/components/charts` — it exists so an agent can reason about the kit's
*identity* without re-reading all of that first.

### Colors

- **`carbon-black`** is the only "primary" this system has — it carries ink,
  headings, and the one filled/solid CTA per screen. There is no separate
  `primary` token by convention; brand-named tokens are used directly (see
  Provenance).
- **`off-white`** is both the text color on emphasis fills (buttons, dark
  surfaces) and the recessed "inset" surface — the same value does two jobs on
  purpose.
- **Surfaces step from `surface-canvas` (page) → `surface-muted` (quiet bands:
  table heads, hover rows) → `surface-raised` (cards, panels, menus)** — an
  ascending ladder, not three unrelated grays. Never use `surface-raised` for
  the page background; it reads too heavy at full bleed.
- **`dusty-mauve`** is the one accent used for *selection state* (toggle
  groups, segmented controls) — not a general-purpose brand color.
- **`success`/`warning`/`error`/`info`** are status roles only. Never repurpose
  them as a generic fifth UI color, and never use `carbon-black`'s role
  (emphasis/CTA) for a status signal.
- **`identity-1`…`identity-6`** tell N things of the *same kind* apart — six
  verticals, six owners, six tenants — where the difference carries no order
  and no status. They are not the chart slots: chart colors are tuned for
  marks the kit compensates for (2–3px strokes, marker rings, a table twin),
  and a 7px dot gets none of that. **Six is the limit** — past six, use a
  label instead of a color. Values lift on dark so slots 2 and 3 land on the
  brand pastels.
- The full chart palette (6 fixed slots, sequential and diverging ramps, strict
  separation rules) is deliberately **not** re-declared here — it's a
  data-visualization concern with its own contrast math, documented in
  `src/styles/charts.css` and enforced by `chart-theme.ts`'s
  `warnOnSeriesCount()`. Read that before touching chart colors.
- Dark theme inverts these by **role**, not by value (`carbon-black` stays the
  ink role but becomes light-on-dark) — see `[data-theme="dark"]` in
  `colors_and_type.css`. Not duplicated here; token *roles* don't change
  between themes, only their resolved value does.

### Typography

Two families, one rule: **Sentient for anything that should read as a
heading, Geist for everything else** — never fake a heading with
`font-weight: 600` on Geist. The tokens above are the **app/dashboard scale**
(`--fs-app-*` in code) — the scale this template's `app/` should use. A
separate, much larger marketing/display scale (`--fs-display` down to
`--fs-caption`) exists in `colors_and_type.css` for full-bleed marketing
pages; it's out of scope here unless `app/` grows a marketing surface.

`label-mono` renders uppercase with tracking in code (`.t-app-mono`) — that
transform isn't representable as a token in this format, so apply it
explicitly wherever `label-mono` is used.

### Layout

8pt spacing scale, `--space-1` (4px) through `--space-10` (128px) — the
`spacing` tokens above. Product surfaces (`AppShell`/`PageHeader` and friends
in `src/components/ui/app-kit.tsx`) read three layout variables with
standalone-safe defaults: `--product-topbar-height` (`0px`), `--content-max`
(`1200px`), `--content-pad` (`--space-6`, 32px) — override these at the host
app's root if nesting under an app-owned topbar.

**Several `layout.css` classes require a specific HTML shape and fail
*silently* without it** — no error, no console message, just a page that
renders wrong in a way that looks like a bug in your own code. Every block
that constrains its markup states the contract above it in `layout.css`; read
that file's header before using the classes without the components. The three
that bite: `.page-header` is a **sibling** of the content, never its parent;
`.data-table-entity` goes on a `<div>` **inside** the cell, never on the
`<td>`; and if an ancestor scrolls, the shell must be `.app-shell--embedded`.
Going through `AppShell`/`PageHeader`/`PageContent`/`TableEntity` from
`app-kit.tsx` handles all three for you.

### Elevation & Depth

Flat-leaning by design. Three soft, low-contrast shadow steps
(`--shadow-sm/md/lg`) exist for cards and overlays that need to lift off the
canvas, but hierarchy is mostly carried by the surface ladder (canvas → muted
→ raised) and hairlines, not by shadow depth. Shadows deepen and tighten
automatically in dark theme — don't hand-tune them per theme.

**Stacking is a scale, not a number you pick.** Every layer comes from
`--z-*` in `colors_and_type.css`:

| Token | Value | For |
|---|---|---|
| `--z-base` / `--z-raised` / `--z-raised-2` | 0 / 1 / 2 | *local* — ordering siblings inside one component |
| `--z-docked` | 5 | docked strips |
| `--z-nav` | 10 | nav bars |
| `--z-sidebar` | 20 | the app shell's sidebar |
| `--z-sticky` | 40 | sticky page headers |
| `--z-affordance` | 60 | hover/focus affordances |
| `--z-scrim` / `--z-modal` | 100 / 101 | modal backdrop and dialog |
| `--z-popover` / `--z-tooltip` | 120 / 130 | popovers, tooltips |
| `--z-toast` | 140 | toasts |
| `--z-skip-link` | 200 | skip link, above everything the kit owns |

**The kit never exceeds `200`, and `300`+ is yours.** Mount an app-owned
overlay there without reading the kit's CSS or grepping for a free number.

### Shapes

- `xs` (4px) — tags, alerts, small chips
- `sm` (8px) — buttons, inputs
- `md` (12px) — cards, panels
- `lg` (20px) — modals, sheets, hero surfaces
- `pill` (999px) — pills, badges, segmented/toggle controls

Square-leaning overall — don't reach for `lg` by default; most UI should sit
at `sm`/`md`.

### Components

The entries above are anchors for the tokens, not the catalog. Every real
component lives in `src/components/ui` (one file per component or tight
family) and `src/components/charts`, following one pattern:

```
Radix primitive + CVA variants (where needed) + brand CSS class + cn() for overrides
```

Components attach a brand class (`.btn`, `.card`, `.checkbox`, `.tabs-*`…)
rather than inline utility classes, so retheming means changing a token or a
class in `ds-components.css`/`primitives.css`/`layout.css`, never touching
component code. Do not run `npx shadcn add` — new components are hand-ported
to this pattern instead.

### Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep one primary (solid, `carbon-black`) call-to-action per region; row
  and card actions use the secondary/"table" variant instead.
- Do use status tokens (`success`/`warning`/`error`/`info`) for status UI —
  never invent a one-off chip color.
- Do restyle globally rather than inline — but **not in this copy.** `src/`
  is the consuming end of the design system, not its origin: a token changed
  in `colors_and_type.css` here works locally and is lost at the next
  promotion. A new token, status, or component convention goes upstream
  first; see [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) and [`README.md`](README.md) §7. A
  product page that carries a brand of its own — the landing — is the other
  case: its theme lives in `app/landing/`, scoped to its own root, and leaves
  the kit's tokens alone.
- Do keep copy out of `src/` components — it goes through the `ui` i18n
  namespace, or arrives as a required prop (`LoginPanel`, `PermissionGate`)
  for anything with product narrative.
- Don't use Tailwind utility classes as the styling layer for a component —
  brand CSS classes are the contract here, Tailwind is not the design system.
- Don't cross a chart past 3 series without the secondary encoding the kit
  ships standard (legend, direct labels, table twin) — see `charts.css`.
- Don't reach for a chart token to tell non-chart things apart — that's what
  `identity-1…6` are for, and six is the ceiling.
- Don't write a raw `z-index`. Take one from the `--z-*` scale, and if the
  layer belongs to the app rather than the kit, start at `300`.
- Don't let `src/` grow a `views/` or `data/` folder, touch
  `window.location`, or reference a business-only i18n namespace — that's
  exactly what the purity checks in `AGENTS.md` exist to catch.

### Provenance

All values in the `kit:` block above were read directly from this repo's own
`src/styles/colors_and_type.css` — nothing measured from a live page,
screenshot, or Figma file, so there's no measured-vs-estimated gap to record.

That file is the single source of truth *for reading*, not for writing:
`src/` is a copy of a system that lives elsewhere, and **this repo carries no
`designSystem` block in any `package.json`** to compare it against. When this
doc and `src/styles/colors_and_type.css` disagree, the CSS wins and this one
is behind.

What's deliberately not duplicated here, and why:

- **`missing-primary` lint warning is expected and accepted.** This system
  organizes color identity around named brand hues (`carbon-black`,
  `dusty-mauve`, …) plus semantic surface/status roles, not the generic
  `primary`/`secondary`/`tertiary` convention — adding an alias just to
  satisfy the convention would mean two names for the same value with no
  behavioral difference, which is the kind of duplication this file is
  trying to avoid.
- **The chart palette, dark-theme value table, and the marketing type scale**
  are described in prose above but not encoded as tokens — each already has
  an authoritative home in code (`charts.css`, `colors_and_type.css`'s
  `[data-theme="dark"]` block, and the `--fs-*` display scale respectively).
  Re-encoding them here would create a second copy that can drift from the
  one actually consumed by the app.
- **The component catalog** (~80 real components) isn't reproduced — the
  `components` tokens above are a representative sample sized to exercise
  every color/type/radius token declared, not an inventory. The real catalog
  is `src/components/ui` and `src/components/charts`, indexed in
  [`README.md`](README.md) §4.
